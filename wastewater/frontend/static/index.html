          How to read the plan

          Two viruses
          Each check reads two CDC datasets held in Zetaris: SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19) and influenza A. The plan shows a block for each, so a quiet COVID reading does not mean a quiet flu season or the other way round.
          Share of samples with the virus
          The fraction of wastewater samples in the period where the virus's RNA was detected. It says how widespread detection is across sampling sites, not how many people are infected.
          Rising, falling or flat
          The change in that share against the preceding period of the same length. Within five percentage points it is reported as flat.
          Concentration
          The average reported concentration. Sites report in different units, so the mean is indicative only when units are mixed.
          How fresh the data is
          Samples are collected, shipped, tested and published, so the newest data is usually about two weeks old.
          Small numbers
          A state with only a handful of samples can show 100% or 0% by chance. The plan always gives the sample count.
